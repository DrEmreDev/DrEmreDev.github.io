Namaz Saat · Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Namaz Saat is an Android app that shows prayer times and notifies you when a prayer time begins. This page is the privacy notice under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): it explains what information the app uses, where it keeps it and who it shares it with. The Turkish version is the original; if the two differ, the Turkish version applies.

In short

  • No ads. No analytics, tracking or advertising tools.
  • No account. The app never asks for your name, email or phone number.
  • The current versions of the app do not connect to the internet and send nothing on their own.
  • Your location is used only on your phone, to find the nearest district. It is not stored, and the app sends it nowhere.
  • The app's information is left out of Android's backup to your Google account and out of transfers to a new phone.
  • The error log stays on your phone. It reaches the developer only if you share it.

1. Data controller and contact

For the KVKK and the GDPR, the data controller is Emre Türkmenoğlu (Türkiye), who develops the app.
Questions and requests about your personal data: emre.bbt01@gmail.com

2. Information kept on your phone

The app keeps the following only on your phone, in storage Android reserves for the app that other apps cannot read:

  • The selected district and up to five recently used districts (the selected one included). Districts chosen by automatic location are added too, so while automatic location is on, the list can show districts you were recently in.
  • Whether automatic location is on, and when the location was last read. The location itself is not kept.
  • Your notification settings for each prayer (sound, reminder in advance) and the notifications planned for the next seven days. The title of a prayer-time notification includes the district name.
  • Your theme choice, and which step of the first launch you reached.
  • For those five districts only, a cache of prayer times and district details: the district's name, province, time zone, the coordinates of the district centre and when it was saved. When a district drops off the list, its details are deleted; old months are removed over time too.
  • The error log (section 5).

The app does not send any of this to the developer or anyone else on its own; the error log leaves your phone only if you share it. Because prayer times and notification settings can hint at your religious practice, the app also keeps this information out of Android's backup to your Google account and out of transfers to a new phone. That is why on a new phone you pick your district again.

3. Location

  • The app reads your location in two cases: when you tap "Konumumu kullan" (Use my location) while picking a district, and while automatic location is on.
  • Picking your district with "Konumumu kullan" turns automatic location on. Turning on Ayarlar › Konum › Otomatik konum (Settings › Location › Automatic location) also reads the location once right away.
  • While automatic location is on, each time the app opens or comes back to the front, it reads the location again if at least half an hour has passed since the last read, and switches to a closer district if you are in one.
  • Location is read only while the app is in use. It is never read in the background, and background location permission is never requested.
  • Approximate location is enough. On Android 12 and later you can choose "Approximate" in the permission dialog.
  • Your location (coordinates) is not stored, not written to the error log, and not sent anywhere by the app. Only the district result is kept: the selected district, up to five recently used districts and when the location was last read.
  • The location comes from your phone's location services. If your phone has Google Play services, the location may come through them. Google runs that service under its own privacy policy.
  • You can turn automatic location off in Ayarlar › Konum › Otomatik konum. Picking a district by hand also turns it off. You can withdraw the location permission at any time in your phone's settings.

4. Notifications

  • Prayer notifications are planned and shown on your phone. They do not come from any server.
  • When the phone restarts or the app is updated, the notifications are set up again. Once a day the app refreshes the notification plan in the background. Nothing is sent while it does.
  • The title of a prayer-time notification includes the district name. You can choose whether notifications show on the lock screen in your phone's notification settings.

5. Error log

  • If something unexpected goes wrong in the app, the time, type and message of the error, and where in the code it happened, are written to a log on your phone. The log is limited to about 64 KB; when it is full, the oldest errors are removed.
  • Before anything is written, numbers with decimals, such as location coordinates, are removed. An error message may contain details such as a district ID.
  • The app never sends the log anywhere on its own, and it has no crash-reporting tool. The log is not included in Android's backup either.
  • If you tap "Hata kaydını gönder" (Send error log) in Ayarlar › Hakkında (Settings › About), or the same button on the error screen, Android's share menu opens. You choose who receives the log and with which app. The shared file also contains the app's version.

6. Information the developer handles

  • What: The developer handles only the emails you send me and their attachments (your email address, the name shown in the email, what you write and, if you send it, the error log), and what Google Play Console shows me (section 7). These are collected electronically when you email me or write a review on Google Play.
  • Purpose: To answer your questions and requests, find and fix errors, and improve the app.
  • Legal basis: KVKK Art. 5(2)(f) (my legitimate interest in solving problems and fixing the app, provided it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms) and, for requests about your KVKK rights, Art. 5(2)(ç) (legal obligation). Under the GDPR, Art. 6(1)(f) and Art. 6(1)(c).
  • Sending these is not required. If you do not, you can use the app just the same; only your question cannot be answered.
  • There is no automated decision-making or profiling. Nothing of a special category, such as religion or belief, is inferred from what you send.
  • How long: I delete error logs once the problem is solved, and in any case no later than six months after I receive them. I delete emails no later than twelve months after the conversation ends. Deleted emails stay in Gmail's trash for 30 more days and are then removed automatically.
  • Who receives it: I do not share or sell what you send me. Emails are kept in Gmail, the email service I use; Google, which runs Gmail, may store them on servers outside Türkiye. If a law requires it, information is given only to the competent public authorities.

7. Internet and third parties

  • The current versions of the app do not connect to the internet and do not ask for internet permission.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, crash-reporting or social media tools. There is no sign-up or sign-in.
  • If you install the app from Google Play, Google handles information such as installs and updates under its own policy. Google Play Console shows me aggregate statistics that do not identify you: install counts and breakdowns by country, device and Android version. If sharing usage and diagnostics data is on in your phone, Google also shows me a technical report when the app crashes or freezes: where in the code it happened, the device model, and the Android and app versions. These reports do not contain your name or account. If you write a review on Google Play, I can see the review, the name Google shows with it and device details, and I can reply.
  • Apps you pick in the share menu (email, messaging) handle what you send under their own policies.
  • The "Gizlilik politikası" (Privacy policy) link in Ayarlar › Hakkında opens the Turkish version of this page (https://dremredev.github.io/namazsaat/gizlilik/) in your browser; this English version is at https://dremredev.github.io/namazsaat/privacy/. These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep technical records of visits (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy statement.

9. Security

Information on your phone is kept in storage Android reserves for the app, which other apps cannot read, and Android protects it with device encryption. The app does not connect to the internet, so there is no transfer to intercept, and the information does not go into backups. I keep what you send me in an email account protected by 2-step verification, and I do not copy it anywhere except to fix the error. If there is a data breach, I will notify Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Board and the people affected, as the KVKK requires.

10. Keeping and deleting

  • Information on your phone stays until you uninstall the app or use Apps › Namaz Saat › Storage › "Clear data" in your phone's settings. Since it is not in any backup, this deletes it completely.
  • The cache holds details for the five most recently used districts only; old months are removed over time.
  • For how long the developer keeps what you send, see section 6.

11. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed; to request information about it; to learn the purpose and whether it is used for that purpose; to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad it was transferred to; to have it corrected if incomplete or wrong; to have it deleted or destroyed under Article 7 of the KVKK; to have such corrections and deletions passed on to those third parties; to object to an outcome against you that results solely from automated analysis; and to claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing. If the GDPR applies to you, you also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interest.

Send your requests to emre.bbt01@gmail.com. I answer requests free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If your request is refused, you find the answer unsatisfactory or you get no answer within 30 days, you can complain to Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Board. If you live in the European Union, you can also complain to the data protection authority of your country.

You can delete the information on your phone yourself (section 10).

12. Children

The app is suitable for users of all ages. It asks no one for personal information and collects none.
